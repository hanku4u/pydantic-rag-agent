# Support FAQ

## Hours

Support is staffed 09:00 to 17:00 UTC on weekdays. Outside those hours, the
on-call rotation handles production incidents only.

## Escalation

A ticket that has not received a response within two hours is escalated to tier 2
automatically. Tier 2 escalations are reviewed at the weekly support meeting.

## Contacting us

Use the support portal for new tickets. Email is fine for follow-ups on an
existing ticket, but it does not create one.
