# NNNN — Short imperative title

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces at play, described neutrally. What constraint forced a decision to be
made at all? What did the alternatives look like? Write this so a reader who
disagrees understands the problem before they see the answer.

Avoid advocacy here. The Context section should be readable by someone who thinks
the Decision is wrong.

## Decision

What was chosen, in the active voice. "We use X", not "X was considered". Be
specific enough that a reader can tell whether a proposed change complies with it.

## Consequences

What follows. Include the costs honestly — a record with no downsides is a record
that has not been thought about.

- **Easier:** what this simplifies.
- **Harder:** what this now makes more awkward.
- **Foreclosed:** what is now impractical without a new decision.
- **Revisit when:** the specific condition that would justify superseding this.
